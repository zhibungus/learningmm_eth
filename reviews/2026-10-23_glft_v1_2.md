## 2. New-code review
* **RNG split: correct** [CONFIRMED].
  * Feed draws now depend only on event times. The order-jitter draws are aligned by order index, not by time. That is acceptable and documented in spec §9.
  * Results are deterministic (bit-exact reproduction above).
* **Multi-seed aggregation: correct** [CONFIRMED by test and by re-deriving the per-seed values in `metrics.json` `base_seeds` / `sensitivity.*.per_seed`].
  * The sd uses ddof = 1.
  * Minor: the mechanism averages drop any mechanism missing in one seed (`report.py:105-106`). For example, `taker` disappears whenever some seeds had no kill. Cosmetic.
* **`--workers` path: correct.**
  * The headline run is (base, run.seed) only (`is_headline`), and only it keeps fills, equity and the file manifest.
  * The output is assembled in config order.
  * `test_backtest_seeds.py:97` checks the job plan.
  * There is still no automated 1-worker vs N-worker equality test. My bit-exact reproduction covers it for now.
* **N1 exit: correct.**
  * One exit order is in flight at a time, so a flip is impossible. The size comes from the strategy's view of q, so it may overshoot by at most a late quote fill (≤ Δ), and the next tick corrects it.
  * During a gap, while prices are missing, the exit waits (test 467).
  * Small: `flat_after_kill_ms` is only measured at the next timer tick, so it reads 1000 / 2000 ms when the fill actually came at about 409 ms. Say "≤" in the docs, or stamp it in `on_fill`.
* **Race classification: correct.**
  * The `replace_rejected: qty_below_filled` branch is now dead in the backtest, because the engine cancels instead. It is kept on purpose for the paper/live adapter. Fine.
  * [SUSPICION] In live trading, a genuine sizing bug that gives `notional_below_min` on an order that also filled within 2 s would be hidden as an explained race. Low risk.

## 3. Interpreting the v1.2 results

### The kills: expected, not a regression [CONFIRMED]
* Base OOS sits **right on the 5 USDT line**. With the kill switched off, the max drawdowns are 5.01 / 5.10 / 5.09 / 5.11 / 5.46 (seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 42).
* My old shared-RNG paths gave 4.70–5.01 for seeds 1–4. Same distribution; the path sd is about 0.3.
* So whether a seed is killed is a coin flip of about 0.1 USDT. 0/4 kills before and 3/4 now are both normal draws.
* The pre-kill strategy logic changed only in rare race handling (no cooldown on race rejects, cancel on qty_below_filled), and the exit change acts only after a kill.
* Seed 1 (dd 5.01, not killed) shows the strategy checks its own delayed, mid-marked equity once per second.

### Does the 5 USDT limit dominate the OOS comparison? Yes, for PnL
**Uncensored OOS** (kill off, 5 seeds; diagnostic only):

| | base | skew_off | fee_shift | gamma_low | gamma_high |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| net, kill off: mean (sd) | **−4.60 (0.27)** | −2.79 (0.62) | −3.24 (0.47) | −3.95 (0.57) | −4.33 (0.27) |
| net in report (kill on) | −4.98 (0.30), 4/5 killed | −2.79, 0/5 | −3.24, 0/5 | −4.15, 2/5 | −4.39, 1/5 |
| mean \|q\| / time at limit | 0.017 / 3.5% | 0.036 / 35% | 0.029 / 17% | 0.025 / 11% | 0.011 / 0.5% |

* **The kill made base about 0.4 USDT worse on average.** It locks in the loss near the day's bottom and pays the taker fee, and the market then partly recovered.
* So base's report PnL is **biased down by the risk limit**, while the unkilled variants are not.
* The ranking still holds: skew_off > fee_shift > gamma_low > gamma_high > base. But the base gap to skew_off shrinks from 2.19 to 1.81.

**How to compare variants when kills happen (spec §9 already says part of this):**
1. Judge H3 and H8 on inventory metrics, not on PnL.
2. For PnL, add an interpretation-only **`no_kill` diagnostic row** (risk limit off) next to the real row. Alternatively, truncate each pair of runs at the earlier kill time.
3. Report the **paired sign count** per seed.
4. Compute time at limit and mean \|q\| over **pre-kill quoting time**. Today the flat post-kill hours dilute them for killed runs (base OOS 0.015 in the report vs 0.017 uncensored).

### H3 (skew controls inventory): HOLDS
* The report's 5 seeds: mean \|q\| 0.019 vs 0.032 (IS) and 0.015 vs 0.036 (OOS); time at limit 2.1% vs 31.7% and 3.5% vs 35.0%.
* That is 10 of 10 seed-periods, the same as my v1_1 probes.
* skew_off's better PnL: 4 of 5 seed-pairs IS (seed 42 is worse, −3.17 vs −3.11), and 5 of 5 OOS. That is the "fewer fills in a fee-negative strategy" effect (660 vs 815 fills IS). It is not a case for dropping the skew.

### H8 (exact fee folding beats the shift): HOLDS
* fee_shift has worse inventory control in 10 of 10: \|q\| 0.029 / 0.029, time at limit 22.9% / 16.8%.
* Its better PnL (4/5 IS, where seed 42 is worse, −3.27; 5/5 OOS) has the same cause as skew_off.

### H6 (not an artefact of fills or latency): HOLDS
* Every variant is negative. No listed variant cuts fills by more than 50%.
* These rows run on 1 seed, and the base seed sd is about 0.3, so differences smaller than about 0.6 are noise. For example, pessimistic_fills IS −3.30 vs base −3.11.
* 4 of 8 H6 runs were killed (higher_latency IS and OOS, pessimistic_fills OOS, no_cross_fills OOS), so OOS magnitudes are censored at about −5.
* The `gamma_low` OOS mean is also kill-driven (2/5 killed): −4.15 with the kill on vs −3.95 with it off.
